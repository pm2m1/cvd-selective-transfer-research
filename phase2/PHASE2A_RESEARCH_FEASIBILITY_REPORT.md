# Phase 2A research feasibility decision

**Date:** 26 October 2026. **Decision:** a new HRS→ELSA study is a credible *research direction*, but Phase-2 model development is **NO-GO today** because eligible event counts, a release-specific common endpoint/feature crosswalk, and external-validation precision have not been established. This is a bounded feasibility decision, not a new model experiment. The closed HAALSI→MHAS test, models, private predictions and lock were not accessed or changed.

### Additional cautions

The deliberately enriched target adaptation samples had 25% reports, versus 7.6% in validation and 7.4% in the locked test. Target-only models' mean validation probabilities were roughly 21–24% under 25+75 fitting, indicating a prior/calibration concern; a mean matching prevalence is not proof of calibration. Death and loss to follow-up were excluded from the original binary endpoint unless an event had already been reported. Such complete-case selection can change the estimand and induce bias. Source-to-target feature comparability, reporting access, survey language and country context can all change predictor/outcome relationships, but Phase 2A cannot attribute the old null finding to one of these mechanisms.

## New independent-data finding

Official documentation identifies **HRS public longitudinal data** and **ELSA UKDS SN 5050** as a plausible new US→England pair. HRS documents separate heart-attack/stroke questions and biomarker/physical-measure products; ELSA has Wave-6 health visits and subsequent waves. Access is registration-based, with additional restrictions for certain biomarker, date and detailed-history products. Official [HRS access/merge guidance](https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/data-products/file-merge-reference) and [ELSA access page](https://www.elsa-project.ac.uk/accessing-elsa-data) define these routes. However, no approved extract or official cross-wave table yet establishes the number of baseline-free, feature-complete people with observed new reports. Thus **the independent external test exists in principle but its scientific/sample-size feasibility is unverified**. [Dataset comparison](PHASE2A_DATASET_COMPARISON.md) records evidence and gaps. CHARLS and SHARE have compound heart-condition questions and cannot be presumed equivalent heart-attack/stroke populations.

## Novelty and preferred direction

Regularized logistic regression, gradient-boosted trees, model updating, CORAL, source-centred penalties, selective fine-tuning and parameter-efficient adaptation are established. The preferred *testable* direction is **uncertainty-aware, view-specific continuous source shrinkage in a compact tabular model**, compared with global shrinkage and strong classical baselines. It directly addresses hard-mask abstention under low event counts and does not presume a neural advantage. Its novelty remains **unproven** until a systematic prior-art check and a mathematically specified rule are completed. A careful result in which ordinary logistic/boosting models win is still valuable as a reproducibility/transportability study. See the [literature review](PHASE2A_LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY_REVIEW.md), [three hypotheses](PHASE2A_NEW_METHOD_CANDIDATES.md) and [proposed protocol](PHASE2A_RECOMMENDED_EXPERIMENTAL_PROTOCOL.md).

## Quantitative feasibility rule

Do not convert whole-wave sample counts into incident events. Use ≥100 events and ≥100 nonevents in the *new external test* only as an initial screen from [Vergouwe et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15845334/), with **≥200 target test events as a provisional design aim**. [Riley et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.9025) show that adequacy depends on desired precision of discrimination and calibration; AP/paired AP contrast precision requires an additional prespecified simulation or paired-bootstrap planning exercise. Positive labels reserved for adaptation and validation are **in addition** to independent test events. A 10-event adaptation arm is exploratory; 25/50-event arms are conditional on observed counts. No HRS/ELSA eligible-event estimate is available yet, so a power or precision claim would be invented.

## Phase 2B entry conditions

1. Acquire HRS/ELSA products through the official routes and document applicable licences; do not use another person's credentials.
2. Produce a release-specific field crosswalk for baseline landmark, doctor-reported heart attack/stroke, death/proxy/attrition, units, timing and person linkage.
3. Report an aggregate-only eligibility waterfall and actual source/target incident event counts for each common/extended feature tier.
4. Complete an external test-size/precision calculation and reserve separate target adaptation and development validation events before a new one-time split.
5. Freeze a bounded candidate method and strong conventional comparators without revisiting the old MHAS test. Do not pursue a neural novelty claim before checking established source-shrinkage and model-updating literature.

**No training, new split or test evaluation was performed in Phase 2A.** The independent data path and scientific question are plausible, but the mandatory quantitative and endpoint gates are open.
