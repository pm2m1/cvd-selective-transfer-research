# Feasibility preparation decision

**CONDITIONAL GO — scientifically defensible survey-report endpoint and count implementation; empirical adequacy remains incomplete. No ML-training GO.**

This decision concerns public preparation, not private empirical acceptance. Exact new eligible populations, MI/stroke events, complete cases and untouched-evaluation support for W6→9 and W4→7 are **PENDING_NEW_AUTHORIZED_LOCAL_EXECUTION**. Neither is claimed larger or better. Prior 8,915 and570 screens are not reused as a sequential waterfall or alternative-cohort counts; their earlier public outputs remain unchanged.

## What is now established

- Official dictionary bytes for the named EUL products are retrieved and fingerprinted; exact per-field codes and absence evidence are machine readable. The W4 EUL interview field is `w4indout`; the older guide's alias is not copied blindly. W9 `hedacmi` is absent and never synthesized.
- Continuing participants can qualify through original lifetime/interval histories. Global absence of hypertension, diabetes or every other condition is not required. Harmonized screening zeros, unselected dummies, skipped branches and unknown follow-up do not become disease-free observations.
- A standalone selected-column reader and pure count engine produce separate MI/stroke states, composite unique-person counts, baseline/outcome/feature flows, and prospective disjoint role-support scenarios. It does not invoke Stage C or load any HAALSI/MHAS/HRS/frozen-test workflow.
- The automatic baseline-feature adapter and separately optional reviewed evidence contract preserve baseline-only information and explicit nurse timing. Later revisions of harmonized diabetes and cross-wave sex backfill are not blindly used as baseline features.
- Both timing policies are prespecified before new empirical counts. The primary approximately-six-year report endpoint and conservative fixed-six-year sensitivity are distinguishable. A questionnaire date-field name is not evidence that the released file includes it.

## Exact unresolved dependencies

1. New independently applicable permission and isolation for this specific protected operation; old consent/download/run records do not automatically satisfy it.
2. Registered hashes and compatible actual clinical products for Harmonized H and Core1–9. No private file presence or header was checked through Codex. The exact frozen distributor archive edition remains unauthenticated; qualified local provenance is not relabeled independent authentication.
3. Two nurse products for automatic complete-case timing availability, or independently reviewed equivalent baseline evidence. Timely measured predictors may remain restrictive; later examinations cannot be backdated to repair that limitation.
4. Actual new count/uncertainty artifacts, reviewed privately with disclosure controls. The code cannot supply those counts without the prohibited-in-Codex microdata operation.
5. HRS source eligibility and interval endpoint support, including the unresolved directly observed MI-negative route, source/target measurement comparability, model-specific precision targets and a genuine independent test protocol before any model comparison.

## What the result would and would not justify

Enough verifiable reports with acceptable feature chronology and distinct development/evaluation reserves could justify planning a new appropriately authorized modeling study. The event goal must follow desired uncertainty, model complexity and ascertainment validity. Neither a software PASS nor reaching200 events makes the study valid automatically.

If report counts are inadequate, retain NO-GO for the tested experiment and assess whether the failure is endpoint evidence, unknown follow-up, measurement chronology or statistical information. If many histories/outcomes remain unknown, that is not proof that every legitimate ELSA design is infeasible. Component-specific populations, new landmarks or new products would require prospectively defined changes; do not choose a new window merely because its count is larger.

If the intended scientific claim requires accurately timed **clinical incident onset** rather than first qualifying survey reports, essential timing/ascertainment evidence is still insufficient. This package cannot certify that claim. Broader heart disease, TIA, prevalent diagnoses, missing negatives or delayed reports must not be used to inflate incidence.

HRS→ELSA remains a feasibility candidate rather than an empirically accepted second transfer-learning pair. Switching to SHARE is not justified by an assumed larger sample alone; it would need its own endpoint, timing, access and event-support audit. No real training or external validation is authorized by this decision.
