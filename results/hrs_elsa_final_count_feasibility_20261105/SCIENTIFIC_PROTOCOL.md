# Two-design count-only feasibility protocol v1

Prepared 5 November 2026. AI-authored, researcher-delegated public methodology. No participant records, private configurations, prior clinical results or authorization receipts were accessed. This is a new prospective count specification; it does not amend a frozen experiment or authorize restricted processing.

## Question, unit and scope

For ELSA Wave6→9 (2012–13→2018–19) and Wave4→7 (2008–09→2014–15), how many distinct baseline MI/stroke-free adults have defensible subsequent personal-interview MI/stroke report ascertainment? Count people, not interviews. The preferred estimand is a **first qualifying personal report of doctor-diagnosed MI or stroke**, after verified absence of the relevant reported histories at baseline. This is not adjudicated clinical onset, a population incidence rate, or survival-adjusted cumulative incidence.

Baseline age is the original Core `indager` (≥50); interview must be completed personal (`w4indout/w6indout=11`, `askpx=0`). This new denominator is defined explicitly rather than assumed identical to the previous screen using harmonized age. The source universe is exact numeric IDAUNIQ linkage within Harmonized ELSA H, with original Core histories. Unmatched Core people are counted privately as linkage-quality exclusions, not silently added as new independent subjects.

## Disease-free proof, including continuing participants

1. Reconstruct separate MI and stroke histories from all available original Core Waves1–baseline. The reader requires the nine registered Core roles to prevent silently omitting earlier histories. Missing person-wave observations remain missing.
2. Establish a completed personal lifetime-list negative at a documented first response: every earlier participation flag is zero, not missing. Wave1 can supply this anchor. Continue through component-specific interval-negative responses referring to the last actual documented interview. Thus continuing participants with reliable Wave1/2/3 histories are admissible; first participation at Wave6 is **not** required.
3. Require a genuinely completed merged condition list. A zero MI/stroke dummy alone is not disease absence. A valid different condition is permitted. Hypertension, diabetes, angina, heart failure, murmur and other identifiable non-endpoint diagnoses do not automatically exclude people. Unclassifiable/irrelevant cardiac answers retain uncertainty.
4. Scan all positional feed-forward slots. A variable called `hedawmi` is not a stand-alone binary MI flag. Confirmation, disputes and component-specific branch-inapplicability are checked. A skip is cleared only within an independent history proof; it never supplies the proof itself.
5. Any prior personal positive/confirmed report excludes its component. Unresolved disputes, proxy positives, unconfirmed prior records, conflicting harmonized positives or incomplete history remain nonqualifying/unknown. Derived first-report-wave zeros and harmonized history zeros never establish absence. Later negatives cannot erase earlier positive or disputed history.

W1/W2 use merged multicoded mention slots; W3 onward use merged condition dummies. Empty lists, DK/refusal, structural inapplicability without a verified route, and tagged missing values are not negatives. W2 documented feed-forward error states remain unknown. A first-personal interview after a previous incomplete/proxy response is not automatically declared a new lifetime interview: absent explicit proof, the history stays unknown. Therefore this is a reproducible sufficient-evidence cohort, not an assertion that every excluded person had disease.

## Follow-up endpoints and time

Retain separate component states before their union. A new positive needs a verified baseline-free component, clear new-report branch, documented interval reference at/after baseline, and qualifying report time. It does **not** require filling every intervening missing diagnosis negative. A negative needs a complete linked negative chain through the closing time. An unresolved first historical appearance or dispute is not repaired by a later new-list selection. Repeated reports count one affected person.

**Primary: approximately-six-year survey window.** The closing wave is9 or7. Every possible duration from the actual baseline/closing month bounds must lie within six calendar years ±183 days. When closing dates qualify, reports through that closing observation are considered. If closing ascertainment is absent, a previously known qualifying positive is retained only when its report precedes the earliest permitted closing time. Nonevents require a qualifying closing personal response and full interval coverage. This is an interval-report estimand with variable individual observation time, not exact six-year clinical risk.

**Secondary: fixed six calendar years.** Preserve month lower/upper bounds; require a positive report by the earliest possible six-year target. An undated report straddling that target is unknown. Negative coverage must reach the latest possible target, using at most183-day-late ascertainment. No midpoint/day15 is manufactured. These results are always reported separately and may differ legitimately. This prospective timing specification is selected from documentation/date resolution before new empirical counts; it does not replace the previous audit's frozen timing or choose whichever produces more cases.

Proxy, partial, institutional, Exit/noninterview and missing responses do not certify personal negatives. Recorded death status without a qualifying event is censored; an earlier qualifying report can remain a case. Exact death/withdrawal dates and reasons are not invented from administrative labels. True withdrawal versus other nonresponse may remain unresolved. No post-W7 outcome can establish the W4→7 endpoint. Original-source revisions or retrospective corrections remain measurement limitations.

## Predictors and partitions

Eight roles stay age, sex, SBP, DBP, BMI, smoking, diabetes, BP treatment. Counts before predictor deletion are mandatory. With the two additional registered nurse sources, the automatic adapter uses baseline Core age/sex, original-wave diabetes history, past-fed-forward/current-wave smoking and medication fields, and published valid nurse SBP/DBP/BMI with visit-date bounds. An optional independently reviewed feature-evidence file can supply a separately supported baseline reconstruction. Measured/examination features must demonstrably precede baseline; same-month uncertain or later nurse measurements cannot be backdated. A new nurse landmark would need a separate disease-free definition and is not implemented here. Predictor missingness never changes an unknown disease outcome to negative. The published BMI may include estimated weight above130kg; retain that measurement limitation rather than claim exclusively direct measurements.

Do not blindly reuse cross-wave `ragender` or retrospectively revised harmonized diagnoses as baseline predictors. The automatic adapter uses original Core sex and diabetes history through baseline, rather than harmonized diabetes zeros revised by later disputes. The H manual documents that medication reports remain as reported despite later diagnosis disputes and smoking ever-history is fed forward from earlier answers. The exact feature sources/transformations are documented separately. If neither registered measurement inputs nor reviewed feature evidence are supplied, complete-case and independent-partition results are explicitly **NOT_COMPUTED**, rather than claimed zero observed support.

For reviewed complete/timely binary-outcome participants, prespecify one outcome-blind SHA256 assignment:50% adaptation,15% stopping,15% calibration,20% evaluation. Same person receives the same role across both windows, avoiding train/test role switching. This is a count scenario, not a test lock or modeling dataset; no participant assignments are written. Untouched-evaluation support additionally requires independently established `prior_model_use=no`; unknown is never treated as unused. Both windows reuse people and cannot become independent replications. Actual external generalization and an untouched test are not established by these counts.

## Decision and privacy

Report ordered baseline exclusions plus overlapping component/history diagnostic marginals, component outcomes, their joint table, unique composite union, feature losses and role-support scenarios. Do not add overlapping losses or MI+stroke counts as if disjoint. Statistical planning uses explicit assumptions; 200 events is not a universal gate. AP/calibration/paired comparison precision cannot be guaranteed from counts alone. HRS source sufficiency and strict observed-negative MI evidence remain separate unresolved requirements.

All detailed counts and even data-conditioned feasibility verdicts stay private pending disclosure review. Execution requires a fresh independently applicable provider/institutional scope check, new purpose-specific authorization and unused output. The inherited one-attempt runtime is preserved byte-for-byte; no historical attempt or authorization is reused. There is no model fitting or automatic subsequent operation. Public principal result: **CONDITIONAL GO for this report-endpoint feasibility investigation, not model training**.

Source locators are in `OFFICIAL_VARIABLE_DICTIONARY.md` and the fingerprinted `official_variable_map.json`. Machine decisions are fixed in `PROTOCOL.json`.
