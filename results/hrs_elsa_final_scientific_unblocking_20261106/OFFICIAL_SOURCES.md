Checked 6 November 2026. Source codes resolve the CSV citations. This is a public documentation review; no dataset edition or actual input availability is certified.

Locator: Section A age; Section B Health p395 Q response definitions. Verification: Fresh official indexed excerpts; full PDF parser failed. Other field identities reuse prior public records; do not substitute V2..

- **HRSPRODUCT** [Gateway Harmonized HRS official product page](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/gateway-harmonized-hrs). Locator: Version D, August 2023; data login boundary. Verification: Fresh public page; no data download.

Fresh official indexed excerpts narrowed the prior HRS documentation limitation: RAND V1 confirms the stroke response/Q distinction and interview-end age field; HRS D confirms mean SBP and BP-treatment field identities. Full PDF parsing still failed, and prior public HRS DBP/BMI construction records are not presented as a fresh complete manual audit. No older HRS B manual, new RAND V2, or current catalogue edition silently replaces a frozen source.

The selected ELSA named Core4/6/7/9 and Nurse4/6 dictionaries, original archived case, field value domains and section fingerprints are preserved in ELSA_DICTIONARY_EVIDENCE.json. This is public metadata, not a source-registration manifest. Wave9 questionnaire appearance of Hedacmi does not overcome its absence in the selected released-variable dictionary.

Open issues remain actual selected-release baseline timing, full source response routing, joint outcome/proxy semantics and private conflict evidence. These cannot be resolved by path existence, a source filename or a public citation.
