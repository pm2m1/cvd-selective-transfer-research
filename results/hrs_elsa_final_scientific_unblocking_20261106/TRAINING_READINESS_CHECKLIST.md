## Integration requirements that must not be approximated

- Exact raw feature order is age, sex, SBP, DBP, BMI, smoking, diabetes, BP treatment. Five encoded views have widths 4/4/2/4/4 after source-only imputation/indicators. The eight-feature network is 8,017 parameters, not the historical ten-feature 8,033 network.
- Development NPZ has exactly 24 arrays: `x`, `y`, Unicode `ids`, Boolean `availability` for six roles: source_train/source_stop/target_fit/target_stop/target_calibration/target_validation. Separate test NPZ has four arrays only. Extra arrays are rejected before payload access.
- Clinical manifests bind exact feature order, endpoint/design strings, role counts/positive counts and membership digests; IDs use source/target namespaces and cannot overlap. Membership hashes prove consistency with supplied metadata, not authentic eligibility or non-use in earlier research.
- `availability=true` means the cell has a justified baseline construct/timing. Eligible item missingness may be NaN and source-imputed. Unknown nurse chronology or an unmeasured unsupported construct must not be relabeled eligible missingness. Baseline age topcoding needs an adopted representation; −7 is not numeric age.
- Only binary, observed outcomes reach modeling. Disputed, unknown, proxy/exit/censored states remain governed upstream; they are not imputed as zero.
- Source-only preprocessing is fixed. Target fit, stopping, calibration and selection are distinct. All their labels count. No test-dependent tuning. Source/target reports remain nonidentical survey constructs.
- Bind reviewed readiness evidence to exact input manifest. Eight readiness flags in the existing template remain false until genuine independent judgments are documented; do not toggle them to obtain PASS.
- Development and held-out evaluation use distinct single-use human authorizations and new destinations. The original diagnostic/count receipts cannot authorize either. A test is not independent merely because arrays are physically separate.

The existing input contract deliberately does not derive real rows from unresolved questionnaire states. Preparing those rows is a later permitted scientific operation, not an integration defect to bypass here.
