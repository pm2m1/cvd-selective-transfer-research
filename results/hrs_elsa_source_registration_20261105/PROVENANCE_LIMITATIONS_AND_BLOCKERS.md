# Provenance limitations and precise remaining blockers

**Historical source authentication: NOT ESTABLISHED. Actual source registration: NOT EXECUTED. Clinical counts: NOT EXECUTED.**

The public record cannot authenticate any of the ten required clinical products. Its two historical hashes identify code; its 30-entry manifest covers public artifacts. No source bytes, private acquisition records or prior private outputs were inspected here.

## What a future successful registration would establish

1. The explicit, independently reviewed local containers had the privately recorded SHA256 values during the operation.
2. Each documented required basename occurred exactly once across those supplied containers; its full member path was retained privately.
3. Selected members passed ZIP decoding/CRC checks and a limited Stata format-header check.
4. SHA256 of each complete uncompressed member equalled its exclusively created private loose-file copy.
5. The new count candidate bound those loose files and the unchanged public scientific contract.

This is a **new qualified local archive-to-member binding**. It is not independent distributor authentication, recovery of an absent historical pin, proof the supplied ZIP was the historical ZIP, proof the archive was lawfully acquired, or proof of scientific adequacy. Arbitrary bytes with plausible filenames/headers cannot be authenticated using names alone. All data-containing containers remain protected, even though registration never parses rows.

## Two transparent modes, neither a publisher claim

- `verify_existing_local_pin`: the operator supplies a separately reviewed local archive SHA256. The registrar requires an exact match. The operator must establish what that local pin identifies and where it came from. It is not labelled a historical archive pin by this tool.
- `first_local_registration`: used when no trustworthy previous clinical container pin exists. No expected hash may be supplied; a new local container identity is computed. The original `5050stata_*.zip` naming selector and explicit `UKDS_SN5050` declaration are checked, but names do not authenticate origin. Independent source-use review must explicitly permit this qualified, unauthenticated tier. If it cannot, **stop before registration**. Do not choose this mode to evade a mismatching known identity.

Missing source-use scope stops before any archive bytes are opened. A wrong supplied pin, unknown basename, duplicate member, unsafe ZIP member, invalid format header, corrupt selected member or affirmative unsupported edition claim stops. The entire container is hashed, but unrelated member contents are not decompressed or copied. CRC is checked for selected members only; the tool does not claim a full-archive decompression test.

## Irreducible remaining dependencies

- Independently available original/reviewed archives and legal/institutional permission covering archive hashing, decompression and full selected-file copying in the isolated environment.
- All ten exact expected basenames actually present uniquely. Core 1–5 availability is not proven by the earlier reader. If any are absent, obtain the correct authorized product and review its compatibility; do not relabel another revision or invent a hash.
- If a truly authenticated historical/distributor identity is required by an external obligation, independently obtain that evidence. This workflow cannot manufacture it.
- A fresh registration-specific operator authorization, new private authorization record and new private output destination. Failed or interrupted outputs remain consumed; any later attempt requires a separately justified fresh authorization and destination.
- A separate count-operation source-use review and authorization after local review of the new manifest/candidate. Registration authority is not count authority. The count candidate is intentionally not directly execution-approved.
- Scientific source-column/revision compatibility remains for the unchanged count reader to validate in a separately authorized count operation. No disease-free eligibility, event count, model feasibility or independent evaluation conclusion follows from registration.

If optional nurse sources or independently supported feature metadata are unavailable, feature completeness and some partition feasibility remain unavailable. Registration does not derive feature metadata, fabricate unknown predictor values or change disease definitions.

## Isolation limitation

The reused runtime rejects agent/CI/noninteractive execution and requires private outputs outside this tool tree with restrictive permissions. It cannot prove that another process, backup client or AI indexer lacks access. The independent operator must establish the actual environment and scope; permission bits and source hashes alone cannot establish isolation or data-use rights. No actual private paths, hashes or generated outputs may be returned to Codex without independently applicable disclosure permission.
